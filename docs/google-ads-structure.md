# Google Ads Structure

This document prepares future Google Ads campaigns for ZES MEDCORP. It does not launch ads, add tracking IDs, or change bidding settings.

## Positioning

Use paid search to capture high-intent technical demand around medical infrastructure, radiology rooms, RF shielding for RMN, radiation protection for CT/RX, medical equipment, IVD/laboratory projects, service, and preliminary project planning.

The strongest message is not generic healthcare marketing. The page promise should be:

- technical evaluation
- preliminary planning
- indicative estimation
- service triage
- infrastructure and equipment coordination

Avoid promising final prices, guaranteed approvals, instant diagnostics, or regulatory certainty.

## Recommended First Campaigns

### 1. Radiology Room Planning

Purpose: Capture users planning CT, RMN, RX rooms or comparing infrastructure requirements.

Ad groups:

- camera RMN
- camera CT
- camera RX
- amenajare radiologie
- radiology room planner

Recommended landing pages:

- `/radiology-room-planner`
- `/calculatoare/cost-camera-rmn`
- `/calculatoare/cost-camera-ct`
- `/services/radiologie`

Primary CTA:

- Planificare radiologie
- Calculați estimarea orientativă
- Solicitați evaluare tehnică

Quality notes:

- RMN ads must mention RF shielding, Faraday cage, HVAC, vibration, and integration.
- CT/RX ads must mention radiation protection, lead shielding, CNCAN, controlled areas, and room layout.
- Do not mix RF shielding and lead/radiation shielding in the same ad promise.

### 2. CNCAN and Radiation Protection

Purpose: Capture high-intent compliance searches for CT/RX/radiology projects.

Ad groups:

- autorizare CNCAN
- protecție radiologică
- ecranare cu plumb
- cameră CT CNCAN
- cameră RX protecție radiologică

Recommended landing pages:

- `/ghiduri/autorizare-cncan`
- `/services/protectie-radiologica`
- `/calculatoare/cost-camera-ct`
- `/knowledge-hub/autorizare-cncan-pas-cu-pas`

Primary CTA:

- Solicitați evaluare tehnică
- Planificare CT / RX
- Structurați propunerea

Quality notes:

- Use careful language: guidance, planning, documentation readiness, technical validation.
- Do not imply ZES can guarantee authorization outcomes.

### 3. RF Shielding / RMN

Purpose: Capture MRI/RMN infrastructure and shielding demand.

Ad groups:

- RF shielding RMN
- cameră Faraday RMN
- ecranare RF
- amenajare cameră RMN
- cost cameră RMN

Recommended landing pages:

- `/services/rf-shielding`
- `/calculatoare/cost-camera-rmn`
- `/ghiduri/cost-camera-rmn`
- `/knowledge-hub/camera-faraday-rmn`

Primary CTA:

- Planificare RMN / RF
- Calculați estimarea orientativă
- Solicitați evaluare tehnică

Quality notes:

- Keep the ad copy focused on RF attenuation, Faraday cage, RF doors, filters, waveguides, penetrations, HVAC, vibration, and equipment integration.
- Do not mention lead shielding as the main RMN requirement.

### 4. Medical Equipment / Imaging / IVD

Purpose: Capture commercial searches around acquisition, integration, planning, and service.

Ad groups:

- aparatură medicală
- aparatură imagistică
- echipamente IVD
- echipamente laborator
- integrare aparatură medicală

Recommended landing pages:

- `/services/aparatura-medicala`
- `/services/imagistica-medicala`
- `/services/ivd-laborator`
- `/calculatoare/cost-echipamente-imagistica`
- `/calculatoare/cost-laborator-ivd`

Primary CTA:

- Analiză proiect
- Calculați estimarea orientativă
- Discutați proiectul

Quality notes:

- Ads should qualify that ZES supports equipment selection, integration, infrastructure readiness, and service planning.
- Avoid broad medical-supply queries unless they are tightly qualified.

### 5. Service and Maintenance

Purpose: Capture urgent or high-value service inquiries.

Ad groups:

- service aparatură medicală
- service CT
- service RMN
- mentenanță aparatură medicală
- service echipamente IVD

Recommended landing pages:

- `/service-diagnostic`
- `/services/service-aparatura-medicala`
- `/calculatoare/service-aparatura`
- `/ghiduri/service-aparatura-medicala`

Primary CTA:

- Evaluați problema service
- Solicitați intervenție
- Discutați proiectul

Quality notes:

- Do not promise immediate intervention unless operational capacity is confirmed.
- Keep language around triage, urgency, downtime risk, and evaluation.

### 6. Project Planning / Turnkey Medical Infrastructure

Purpose: Capture users planning clinics, modernization, medical fit-out, or broader project budgets.

Ad groups:

- construcții clinici medicale
- amenajări medicale
- cost clinică medicală
- modernizare clinică
- proiect medical turnkey

Recommended landing pages:

- `/proposal-builder`
- `/ai-project-advisor`
- `/calculator-proiect-medical`
- `/services/constructii-medicale`
- `/services/amenajari-medicale`
- `/ghiduri/cost-clinica-medicala`

Primary CTA:

- Generați propunerea preliminară
- Începe analiza preliminară
- Calculați estimarea orientativă

## Location Targeting

- Romania

## Launch Notes

Before launch:

- Activate GA4/GTM IDs through environment variables only.
- Define conversion events in GTM/GA4.
- Test lead forms on production.
- Verify no PII is sent to analytics.
- Use Search Console and landing page diagnostics to catch index/canonical issues.
