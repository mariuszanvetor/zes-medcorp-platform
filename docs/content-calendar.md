# 3-Month Content Calendar

Purpose: guide post-launch publishing for ZES MEDCORP with useful, high-intent content. This calendar balances SEO, authority, lead generation, and trust. It does not require creating all pages at once.

Recommended cadence:

- 1 substantial article or guide per week.
- 1 smaller FAQ/comparison/update per week when capacity allows.
- 2 LinkedIn posts per week.
- 1 lead magnet planned or drafted per month.
- Calculator/tool expansions only after the supporting content is strong.

## LinkedIn Cadence

- Tuesday: educational or technical clarification.
- Thursday: practical project insight, checklist, or decision-making advice.

Month 1 themes:

- RF vs plumb.
- CT/RX/CNCAN.
- Equipment selection and room readiness.

Month 2 themes:

- IVD/laboratory planning.
- Modernization and downtime.
- Service and preventive maintenance.

Month 3 themes:

- DSP and clinic planning.
- RMN vs CT infrastructure.
- Replacement and lifecycle planning.

Post format rules:

- One idea per post.
- No fake client references.
- No invented numbers.
- End with a practical question or link to a relevant guide/tool.

## Measurement Plan

Track monthly:

- Organic impressions and clicks per cluster.
- Tool starts and completions.
- Lead form submissions by source.
- CTA clicks from articles/guides.
- Pages with high impressions but low CTR.
- Pages with good engagement but weak conversion.
- Search terms that reveal missing content.

Review decisions:

- Expand pages that attract qualified traffic.
- Improve pages with traffic but low conversion.
- Delay tools without clear commercial behavior.
- Do not publish pages just because a keyword exists.
