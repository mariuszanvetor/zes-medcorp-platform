# Editorial Strategy

Goal: build ZES MEDCORP into a trusted Romanian authority for medical infrastructure, equipment, imaging, IVD, shielding, service, and technical planning.

This is not a plan for publishing hundreds of thin pages. It is a three-month foundation for useful, commercially relevant content that helps buyers make better decisions and gives ZES better-qualified conversations.

## Target Audience Groups

Clinic owners and investors:

- Need investment clarity, timeline expectations, technical risk awareness, and a reliable project partner.
- High-value actions: use calculator, request consultation, start Proposal Builder.

Medical managers and operations teams:

- Need continuity, service planning, equipment uptime, and practical workflow advice.
- High-value actions: service diagnostic, maintenance inquiry, equipment integration discussion.

Doctors opening or expanding practices:

- Need accessible explanations of construction, DSP, radiology, equipment choices, and budget sequence.
- High-value actions: AI Project Advisor, clinic cost guide, contact request.

Procurement and finance stakeholders:

- Need comparison logic, budget bands, lifecycle cost, service implications, and risk justification.
- High-value actions: equipment guide, ROI calculator later, Proposal Builder.

Technical consultants, architects, and project managers:

- Need terminology clarity, shielding separation, CNCAN/DSP distinctions, room requirements, and coordination checkpoints.
- High-value actions: radiology planner, technical guides, direct consultation.

Hospital or department decision-makers:

- Need modernization planning, imaging equipment integration, downtime control, and phased execution.
- High-value actions: modernization content, service diagnostic, contact.

## Content Pillars

### Constructii medicale

Why it matters:

- Clinics and medical spaces need early planning across architecture, technical infrastructure, flows, equipment, and authorizations.

Lead type:

- Clinic founders, investors, expansion projects, medical entrepreneurs.

Best CTAs:

- AI Project Advisor, Calculator proiect medical, Proposal Builder, contact.

### Radiologie / CNCAN

Why it matters:

- CT, RX, fluoroscopy, and related radiology projects require careful planning around radiation protection, controlled areas, documentation, and CNCAN considerations.

Lead type:

- Radiology clinics, hospitals, imaging centers, investors with CT/RX projects.

Best CTAs:

- Radiology Room Planner, cost camera CT calculator, CNCAN guide, contact.

### RF shielding / RMN

Why it matters:

- RMN rooms require RF integrity, Faraday cage planning, RF doors, filters, penetrations, HVAC coordination, vibration considerations, and equipment coordination. This is separate from lead/radiation shielding.

Lead type:

- MRI/RMN providers, imaging centers, technical teams with room readiness concerns.

Best CTAs:

- Radiology Room Planner, cost camera RMN calculator, RF shielding service page.

### Imagistica medicala

Why it matters:

- CT, RMN, RX, ultrasound, and multi-equipment imaging projects depend on room readiness, service strategy, procurement timing, and integration.

Lead type:

- Equipment acquisition projects, imaging expansion, replacement projects.

Best CTAs:

- Imaging equipment calculator, Proposal Builder, contact.

### IVD / laborator

Why it matters:

- Laboratory projects need equipment selection, workflow planning, calibration, validation, service continuity, and infrastructure readiness.

Lead type:

- Lab investors, clinics adding lab capability, procurement teams.

Best CTAs:

- IVD calculator, IVD service page, Proposal Builder.

### Service aparatura

Why it matters:

Lead type:

Best CTAs:

### Proiectare medicala

Why it matters:

Lead type:

Best CTAs:

- AI Project Advisor, Proposal Builder.

### Autorizare si conformitate

Why it matters:

Lead type:

Best CTAs:

### Achizitie echipamente

Lead type:

Best CTAs:

### Modernizare clinici

Why it matters:

Lead type:

- Established clinics and hospitals with renovation or replacement needs.

Best CTAs:

- modernization guide, service diagnostic, contact.

### Planning tools and calculators

Why it matters:

- Calculators and diagnostic tools turn research traffic into structured inquiries and improve lead context.

Lead type:

- High-intent users who need budget, timeline, risk, or technical next-step clarity.

Best CTAs:

## Commercial vs Informational Balance

Recommended first three months:

- 40 percent commercial investigation content.
- 35 percent technical planning and compliance content.
- 15 percent service/problem content.
- 10 percent authority commentary and thought leadership.

Reason:

- The site already has broad service coverage. Growth should focus on searchers who are close to a project decision, while still building trust through useful technical explanations.

## Authority-Building Strategy

Publish fewer pieces, but make each one operationally useful:

- Explain what decisions depend on.
- Separate common confusions: RF vs plumb, DSP vs CNCAN, RMN vs CT/RX.
- Show practical sequencing.
- Include checklists and missing-data sections.
- Use clear disclaimers where estimates are not offers.
- Avoid fake certainty, fake stats, fake testimonials, and invented case studies.

Every authority page should answer:

- What is the project type?
- What can go wrong?
- What needs to be validated?
- What decisions affect budget and timeline?
- Which ZES service or tool helps next?

## Conversion Strategy

Primary conversion paths:

- High-intent cost page -> calculator -> lead capture.
- Technical guide -> relevant planner -> consultation.
- Service content -> contact or service diagnostic.
- Equipment content -> Proposal Builder or contact.
- Article -> related service -> tool -> lead.

CTA hierarchy:

- Use one primary CTA per page section.
- Keep CTAs contextual.
- Avoid button spam.
- Avoid generic "contact us" when a more precise next step exists.

Recommended CTA mapping:

- Clinic construction topics: AI Project Advisor, Calculator proiect medical.
- RMN topics: Radiology Room Planner, cost camera RMN calculator.
- CT/RX/CNCAN topics: Radiology Room Planner, cost camera CT calculator.
- Equipment topics: Proposal Builder, imaging equipment calculator.
- IVD topics: IVD calculator, contact.
- Service topics: Service Diagnostic, service calculator.

## Internal Linking Strategy

Service to article:

- Each service page should link to 2-3 genuinely relevant articles or guides.
- Example: RF shielding service links to RF vs plumb and cost camera RMN.

Article to service:

- Every article should include contextual service links where the reader is evaluating implementation.
- Avoid blocks of unrelated service links.

Guide to calculator:

- Cost and planning guides should link to calculators in the first half of the page.
- The calculator should be the action, not decorative navigation.

Calculator to consultation:

- Calculator results should recommend a next action based on risk/complexity.
- Link to Proposal Builder or contact only after showing a useful result.

Article to article:

- Use 2-4 internal article links when they deepen the topic.
- Avoid repeating the same exact anchor text across every page.

## Content Quality Rules

- Do not publish AI-spam pages.
- Do not keyword stuff headings or footers.
- Keep technical accuracy above ranking ambition.
- Clearly separate RF shielding for RMN from ecranare cu plumb/protectie radiologica for CT/RX.
- Clarify that CNCAN relates to radiation/radiology, not RF shielding by itself.
- Keep budget language orientative unless validated commercially.
- Do not invent statistics, certifications, testimonials, clients, or case studies.
- Prefer practical checklists, sequencing, risks, and decision criteria.
- Use a human editorial pass before publishing.
- Update articles when regulations, standards, supplier realities, or service offerings change.
