# Lead Targeting Strategy

This document outlines who ZES MEDCORP should target through LinkedIn and manual outreach.

## Target company types

- Private clinics
- Imaging centers
- Diagnostic centers
- Laboratory operators
- Hospital departments
- Medical construction firms
- Healthcare architects / project consultants
- Procurement teams
- Private equity or investor-backed healthcare projects

## Target roles

- Clinic owner
- General manager
- Medical director
- Radiology manager
- Operations manager
- Procurement lead
- Project manager
- Architect / technical consultant
- Facility / maintenance lead
- Hospital administrator

## Priority segments

1. RMN projects
2. CT / radioprotection projects
3. Imaging replacement or modernization
4. IVD / laboratory projects
5. Service / downtime / continuity problems
6. New clinic planning

## Relationship-first approach

- Start with useful content.
- Offer a calculator, glossary term, comparison page or planning page.
- Only move to Proposal Builder or Project Intake when the project is real enough.
- Keep the first conversation short and technical.

## How to identify serious prospects

Signals of real intent:

- They mention a modality, room, or service type.
- They ask about budget, timeline, or authorization.
- They already have a site, space, or equipment shortlist.
- They ask about downtime, service, or integration.
- They are willing to share a basic project context.

Signals of low priority:

- No project type.
- No timeline.
- No budget context.
- Purely exploratory with no next step.
- Requests for generic prices without room or equipment context.

## Daily outreach volume

Recommended starting point:

- 5 to 10 highly relevant manual outreach messages per day
- 5 to 10 connection requests per day

This should stay personal. If volume goes up, quality usually goes down.

## Transition path

1. LinkedIn conversation
2. Relevant calculator, glossary, comparison or planning page
3. Project Intake for structure
4. Proposal Builder for preliminary technical organization
5. Direct consultation

## Best CTA for each audience

- Clinic owner: Project Intake or Proposal Builder
- Imaging center: RMN / CT calculator or Radiology Room Planner
- Architect: glossary or comparison page first
- Service lead: Service Diagnostic
- Laboratory lead: IVD calculator

## What not to do

- Do not send mass messages.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Do not promise final engineering approval.
- Do not push Proposal Builder too early.
- Do not claim that calculators are final pricing tools.
