Generated: 2026-06-08T08:31:16.850Z

Scope: independent audit of a stratified random sample of 50 products from the current 500-product batch. Existing A/B product grades were ignored. No products were modified, no deploy was performed, and no indexation/sitemap changes were made.

## Method

- Sampled every major category at least once, then filled to 50 products using a seeded random draw.
- Scored each product from 0-10 on commercial quality, product quality, SEO quality, translation quality, specification quality and trustworthiness.
- Flags were assigned for awkward Romanian, machine/template copy, weak specifications, poor titles/slugs, category mismatch, thin content and suspicious source-table wording.
- This is deliberately harsher than the automated ready gate and reflects buyer/SEO/UX judgment.

## Top Strengths

- Product page structure is consistent: image area, CTAs, support/service notes and related product/service sections are generally present.
- Most sampled products have verified local images and no visible source/import/review metadata.
- Slugs generally preserve product codes and are usable as stable catalog URLs.
- Several categories have commercially useful names after repair, especially emergency, patient-care, medical furniture and sterilization examples.

## Top Weaknesses

- Many descriptions are still templated rather than truly buyer-specific; they explain the ZESCORP quotation process more than the product itself.
- Specification depth is the biggest weakness. Many products have only 3-5 meaningful specs, often dimensions/weight/category rather than real technical parameters.
- Some titles remain acceptable but not polished enough for indexation at scale, especially accessory rows, vague configuration rows and model-only rows.
- Some Romanian wording is functional but still mechanical; it needs manual editorial polish before SEO indexation.
- Category confidence is uneven for small accessories and source-table rows where the original catalog context is weak.
