## Harsh Conclusions

- Image recovery improved coverage, but image presence alone is not enough for indexation.
- Many pages still expose catalog-table DNA: long code lists, generic category-driven descriptions, thin buyer context, or weak technical differentiation.
- Standalone indexing should be restricted to products with natural Romanian titles, meaningful descriptions, real specifications and strong buyer utility.
- Accessory and consumable rows are especially risky: many are better used as category-grid inventory or quote-request support, not indexable landing pages.
- Before mass indexation, add a stricter public-index gate that rejects template phrases, code-list titles, low-spec products and pages without product-specific procurement value.
