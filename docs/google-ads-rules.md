# Google Ads Rules

Use these rules before launching any paid campaign for ZES MEDCORP.

## Core Principles

- Be specific, technical, and commercially clear.
- Match each ad group to a relevant landing page.
- Use consultative language: evaluation, planning, preliminary estimate, technical validation.
- Avoid fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake guarantees, and exact price promises.
- Keep RF shielding and radiation protection separate.
- Do not imply ZES is a clinic or provides patient-facing medical services.

## Claims to Avoid

Do not use:

- guaranteed authorization
- guaranteed CNCAN approval
- guaranteed DSP approval
- lowest price
- exact cost without analysis
- instant final offer
- certified if a certification is not documented
- emergency intervention guaranteed
- best in Romania
- AI diagnosis
- automated engineering approval

Use safer language:

- orientare preliminară
- analiză tehnică
- evaluare aplicată
- buget orientativ
- planificare cameră
- pași recomandați
- validare pe documentație

## Technical Accuracy Rules

### RMN / MRI

Allowed themes:

- RF shielding
- Faraday cage
- RF doors
- filters and waveguides
- penetrations
- RF attenuation
- HVAC
- vibration
- equipment integration

Avoid:

- making lead shielding the main RMN requirement
- saying CNCAN applies to RF shielding itself

### CT / RX / Fluoroscopy

Allowed themes:

- radiation protection
- lead-lined walls
- lead doors
- lead glass
- controlled areas
- CNCAN considerations
- room layout
- equipment integration

Avoid:

- presenting RF shielding as the main CT/RX requirement
- implying authorization outcomes are guaranteed

### IVD / Laboratory

Allowed themes:

- workflow
- analyzers
- sample handling
- calibration and validation planning
- integration
- service continuity

Avoid:

- patient diagnostics claims
- performance guarantees not backed by vendor documentation

## Landing Page Rules

Every ads landing page should have:

- clear H1 aligned with the query
- one obvious first action
- visible trust or expectation copy
- no exact final prices
- no fake testimonials or logos
- mobile-readable hero
- clear path to contact or lead capture

Preferred CTA language:

- Solicitați evaluare tehnică
- Discutați proiectul
- Calculați estimarea orientativă
- Planificare radiologie
- Planificare RMN / RF
- Planificare CT / RX
- Evaluați problema service
- Generați propunerea preliminară

Avoid:

- Cumpără acum
- Ofertă garantată
- Preț final instant
- Urgență azi
- Cel mai bun preț

## Keyword Safety

Avoid broad keywords that attract patient traffic:

- clinică medicală programare
- doctor
- medic
- tratament
- simptome
- analize medicale preț pacient
- RMN preț investigație
- CT preț pacient

Use qualified phrases:

- amenajare cameră RMN
- cost cameră CT
- protecție radiologică cameră RX
- RF shielding RMN
- echipamente IVD laborator
- service aparatură medicală

## Negative Keywords

Review search terms weekly at launch. Start with negatives such as:

- job
- angajare
- curs
- facultate
- referat
- gratuit
- gratis
- download
- pacient
- programare
- simptom
- tratament
- medic
- doctor
- second hand, unless used equipment becomes a deliberate campaign

## Conversion Quality Rules

Do not optimize on clicks alone.

Use primary conversion bidding only after:

- form submit events are tested
- duplicate firing is checked
- lead quality is reviewed manually
- search terms are filtered
- landing pages are checked on mobile

Tool completions and CTA clicks are useful signals, but lead form success should remain the primary commercial conversion.

## Review Cadence

First two weeks:

- search terms twice per week
- conversion event QA weekly
- landing page engagement review weekly
- negative keyword expansion weekly

After stabilization:

- search terms weekly
- landing page quality monthly
- conversion quality monthly
- ad copy and CTA review monthly
